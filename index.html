About Us

Isimilo se Ntombi is a non-profit organisation in Gauteng that walks alongside young maidens on a journey of purity, dignity, and purpose, from their school years until they graduate and step into adulthood.

We believe every girl deserves to grow up knowing her worth. Rooted in the traditions of our elders, including the pride and discipline celebrated at the Umhlanga Reed Dance, we guide young women to honour themselves, their families, and their communities.

Founders: Khanyisile Magasela and partners.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To guide and mentor young girls in Gauteng to value their purity, stay committed to their education, and grow in self-respect, discipline and cultural pride until they complete school, with the support of their parents, elders and community.

Our Vision

A generation of confident, dignified and purposeful young women who know their worth, honour their heritage, and rise as leaders in their families and communities.
